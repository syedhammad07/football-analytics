    How the match % works. Each stat becomes a percentile (0 to 100) among players in the same position with enough minutes (). Passing and touches are scaled by 50 ÷ team possession and defending by 50 ÷ the opponents' possession, so a Man City midfielder and a relegation-battler are compared fairly. The match % is 100 minus the average gap between the two players' percentiles across all 27 stats, so it reflects level as well as style.

    Two sets of data. Premier League uses the league's official stats (Opta), for 2025/26 and this season so far. Top 5 leagues uses one source for all five leagues (SofaScore season totals, 2025/26), so players in different countries are measured the same way; three stats differ slightly between the two (shots inside the box, big chances created and final-third passes replace box touches, through balls and forward passes), and team possession is estimated from passes.

    Clubs now come from every summer 2026 transfer and loan listed for English, Spanish, Italian, German and French football, plus promotion and relegation. Values are Transfermarkt estimates from , before the window, so a player who has just moved for a big fee may be worth more now.
