Midfield Matchup

Compare two, three or four Premier League midfielders, one season against another or whole careers. In a single season every bar is a percentile against all midfielders who played __MIN__+ minutes that season, so a 1995 season and a 2023 season are each judged against their own league. Careers are ranked against every midfielder with 4,500+ Premier League minutes. Full stats start in 2008/09; before that the league only recorded goals, assists and minutes.

Settle a debate

          A midfielder at a team that has the ball 65% of the time makes more passes and fewer tackles than he would elsewhere. With this on, passing, touches and possession lost are scaled down for teams with more of the ball, and tackles, interceptions and recoveries scaled up, so players at different teams are compared fairly. Style match, roles and similar players always use the adjusted numbers.

                  Summary

                  Roles are learned from 60 well-known seasons, such as Kanté 2016/17 as a 6, Henderson 2019/20 as an 8, De Bruyne 2019/20 as a 10 and Hazard 2014/15 as a wide attacker. Each player is matched to the role whose style his profile is closest to, whatever his overall level.

                  Data: official Premier League stats (Opta) from premierleague.com, __NROWS__ midfielder seasons with __MIN__+ minutes: __NDETAIL__ from 2008/09 with full stats and __NCLASSIC__ classic-era seasons (1992/93 to 2007/08) with goals and assists from premierleague.com and minutes from pssguy/epldata. A midfielder is a player listed as one in his club's squad. Possession lost is reversed, so a higher bar means he lost the ball less. Team possession: official Premier League team stats, used for the adjustment from 2008/09. Dribble success needs 15+ attempts. n/a = the stat isn't recorded for that season.
